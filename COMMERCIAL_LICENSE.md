Commercial licenses are negotiated for the particular product, company, and use
case. Terms may include one or more of:

- revenue share;
- annual or monthly subscription;
- usage-based fees;
- per-seat or per-deployment fees;
- support, warranty, or indemnity terms.

This document is an invitation to negotiate. It is **not** a commercial license
and grants no commercial rights. No percentage, price, or commercial permission
exists until both parties execute a separate written agreement.

Examples that normally require a commercial license include:

- using Anvil in a for-profit company's internal operations;
- offering Anvil or a modified version to paying customers;
- embedding Anvil in a paid product or service;
- operating a hosted or managed Anvil service;
- using Anvil to develop, train, evaluate, or deliver a commercial product.

Whether a specific use is permitted under the public license depends on the
license text and applicable law. Obtain legal advice for your situation.

## Request a license

Contact the copyright holder through the
[ssgaur GitHub profile](https://github.com/ssgaur) with:
